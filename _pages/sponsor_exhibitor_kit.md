## Exhibitor Guidelines

### 1. Booth Dimensions

Custom-designed booths must fit within the allocated space:

- **Diamond Sponsorship:** 6 meters long by 2 meters deep by 2 or 2.5 meters high
- **Platinum, Gold, Silver & Bronze ad-ons Sponsorship:** 3 meters long by 2 meters deep by 2 or 2.5 meters high

No extensions beyond these dimensions are permitted.

### 2. Placement of Materials

All marketing materials, including banner stands, promotional items, TVs or monitors must remain inside the designated booth space. Any items placed outside the booth will be requested to be moved inside or removed completely.

### 3. Audio/Visual Restrictions

No professional video recording or photography is allowed. Microphones, speakers, or amplified sound systems are prohibited.

### 4. Food & Beverage

Any food or beverage offered at your booth must have prior approval from ACL. (ACL/Venue preferred vendor must be used for any approved F&B. No outside food allowed.) Alcoholic beverages are not permitted unless explicitly authorized.

### 5. Safety & Compliance

Booth structures must be stable and comply with venue safety regulations. Electrical equipment must be certified and used with proper surge protection. No open flames, hazardous materials, or items that could obstruct aisles.

### 6. Staffing & Conduct

Booths must be staffed during all exhibit hours. Exhibitors should maintain professional behavior and avoid obstructing neighboring booths.

### 7. Conflicting Events

Sponsor agrees not to host or sponsor any separate event on the evening of the conference's social event that could reasonably be expected to compete for attendee participation.

### 8. Giveaways & Promotional Items

All giveaways must comply with local laws and event policies. No items that create excessive noise, mess, or disruption are allowed.

### 9. Setup & Dismantling

Exhibitors must adhere to the official setup and teardown schedule. Early dismantling before the event closes is not permitted.

### 10. Liability

Exhibitors are responsible for their property and equipment. The event organizer and venue are not liable for loss, theft, or damage.

For Hungexpo Rules and Regulations click [Here](https://2026.emnlp.org/assets/images/sponsors/Hungexpo Rules & Regulations.pdf).

## Custom Booth Builds using an EAC

If you choose to use your own EAC (Exhibitor Appointed Contractor) to build your booth, please note that there are certain rules and regulations.

EAC exhibitors will need to submit the following documentation to Hungexpo via email — **Zita Cserepkei (cserepkei.zita@hungexpo.hu)** — no later than **October 8**:

- Site plan (designating the number of neighbouring stands)
- Furnished, dimensioned floor plans (including the names of the installed materials)
- Facade drawings and typical cross-sections (including designated height)
- Electricity and water supply installation site (if relevant)
- Rigging plan (if relevant)
- Technical description
- Architect's statements featuring the name and signature of the architect
- Fire safety classification of construction products (especially floor and walls)
- Electrical equipment statement (need to be filled and signed by the exhibitor)

A few examples of the above-mentioned can be accessed via the following link: [Click Here](https://2026.emnlp.org/assets/images/sponsors/EAC Forms - Samples.pdf) for exhibitors to see what we usually require.

Please note that EAC exhibitors will need to pay a one-time waste removal fee for the disposal of regular waste and packaging materials accumulated during set-up and dismantling. The order form is below.
