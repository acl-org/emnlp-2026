## Event Details

- **Date:** Saturday, October 24, 2026
- **Time:** 18:00–20:00
- **Location:** Hungexpo Budapest Congress and Exhibition Center
  Albertirsai út 10
  H-1101 Budapest, Hungary
- **Attire:** Business Casual

Begin your EMNLP 2026 experience by joining colleagues from around the world at the official Welcome Reception.

Reconnect with friends and collaborators, meet new members of the natural language processing community, and expand your professional network in a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere before the technical program begins.

Registered attendees will enjoy light appetizers and hosted beverages while connecting with researchers, industry professionals, exhibitors, sponsors, and members of the ACL community.

Whether this is your first EMNLP or your twentieth, the Welcome Reception is the perfect opportunity to start the conference by making new connections and celebrating the beginning of an exciting week in Budapest.

- **Food:** Light Appetizers
- **Beverages:** Beer, Wine & Non-Alcoholic Beverages
- **Included With:** Eligible conference registrations

## Arriving at the Welcome Reception

To help make your arrival as smooth as possible, we encourage attendees to take advantage of early check-in before the reception begins.

Conference check-in opens at **14:00 (2:00 PM)** on Saturday, October 24.

Picking up your badge early will allow you to avoid lines and head directly to the Welcome Reception at 18:00.

### Metro

The easiest way to reach Hungexpo by public transportation is via the Budapest Metro **M2 (Red Line)** to **Örs vezér tere Station**.

From the station, attendees may board the complimentary EMNLP shuttle or enjoy a short walk to the venue.

For metro routes, schedules, and fares, visit the Budapest Transport Centre (BKK) website: <https://bkk.hu/en/>

### Complimentary EMNLP Shuttle

A complimentary EMNLP shuttle will operate between Örs vezér tere Metro Station (M2 – Red Line) and the Hungexpo Budapest Congress Center before and after the Welcome Reception.

A detailed shuttle schedule will be posted closer to the conference.

### Uber & Taxi

Uber, Bolt, licensed taxis, and other rideshare services are readily available throughout Budapest.

**Recommended Drop-Off Location:** Hungexpo Budapest Congress Center – Gate III (Gate 3)

## First-Time Attendees

If this is your first EMNLP conference, we encourage you to arrive early, explore the venue, meet fellow attendees, and stop by the Registration Desk if you have any questions. The Welcome Reception is one of the best opportunities to make new professional connections before the technical sessions begin.
