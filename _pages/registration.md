EMNLP 2026 Registration is scheduled to open on Monday August 10, 2026

Welcome to the registration page for the 2026 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP 2026), taking place October 24–29, 2026, in Budapest, Hungary.

Whether attending in person or virtually, this page provides information on registration categories, pricing, paper presentation registration, deadlines, and conference policies.

Please note: Conference Registration and Paper Presentation Registration are separate requirements. (Paper registrations occur within the conference registration and is not a separate registration)

## Registration Categories

Student
For attendees currently enrolled in an undergraduate, master's, doctoral, or equivalent academic program. Student verification is required by uploading either a current Student ID showing the enrollment period or an official acceptance or enrollment letter from the institution.

Academic
For attendees employed by universities, colleges, government agencies, government research laboratories, nonprofit organizations, or academic research institutions, including Professors, Associate Professors, Assistant Professors, Lecturers, Postdoctoral Researchers, Research Scientists, Research Fellows, Academic Staff, and employees of nonprofit or government research institutions.

Industry
For attendees employed by commercial, for-profit, private sector, or industry organizations, including AI Researchers, Research Scientists, Software Engineers, Machine Learning Engineers, Data Scientists, Product Managers, Technical Program Managers, Consultants, Directors, Executives, and other industry professionals.

## Accepted Paper Information

All individuals participating in EMNLP 2026 must complete either an **In-Person Conference Registration or a Virtual Conference Registration**.

Conference Registration and Paper Presentation Registration are separate requirements. (Paper registrations occur within the conference registration and is not a separate registration)

## Paper Presentation Registration Requirements

All accepted Main Conference, Findings, Computational Linguistics (CL), Transactions of the ACL (TACL), Industry Track, Demo, and Workshop papers must be registered, and the applicable Paper Presentation Registration Fee paid by the applicable Paper Registration Deadline to remain in the conference program and proceedings.

## Exceptions

**Accepted Findings papers** that are not being presented are not required to pay the Paper Presentation Registration Fee and will remain in the proceedings. Authors attending the conference must still complete a conference registration.

**Shared Task Workshop papers** are not required to pay the Paper Presentation Registration Fee. However, any author or designated presenter attending the conference must complete a conference registration.

## Cancellation, Attendance Format Changes & Refund Policy

The Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL) is committed to supporting our attendees while ensuring fairness and predictability in managing event registrations. Please review our Cancellation, Change & Refund Policy carefully before completing your registration.

### Change Fees

A US$25 administrative change fee applies to the following registration changes:

- Switching from in-person to virtual
- Switching from virtual to in-person (if capacity allows)

After October 8, 2026, presentation formats are final and no additional presentation changes will be permitted.

If your visa has not yet been approved, ACL recommends registering as a Virtual Presenter. Once your visa is approved, you may request a change to In-Person Presentation, subject to conference deadlines and scheduling availability.

### Cancellation Deadlines & Fees
On or Before September 24
- Full refund less US $50 processing fee

September 25 – October 1
- Cancellation permitted
- US $200 cancellation fee
- OR convert to Virtual Attendance at no additional charge

October 2 – October 8
- No cancellation refunds
- May convert to Virtual 
Registration changes are permitted only between equivalent registration types. Registrants may not downgrade from one registration product to another after purchase.
Refunds will be calculated using the equivalent registration category only. Registrations may not be converted to a different registration product or package.

After October 8
- No refunds
- No presentation changes
 
Exceptions
 
All exceptions must be submitted in writing with supporting documentation for review and approval by ACL.
